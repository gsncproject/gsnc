Maddie is rumored to be the spirit of a 1920s "lady of the evening" or prostitute who lived and worked within the secret bordello operating inside [The Shanley Hotel](https://thehauntedshanleyhotel.com/our-history/). While historical records of individual workers from the Prohibition era are heavily obscured, she is immortalized in local paranormal culture as a permanent entity attached to her former room. Guests who stay in her designated room frequently experience her playful and occasionally provocative presence.
