# Goatman's Bridge
**Location**: Old Alton Road, Denton / Copper Canyon, Texas
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Iron Truss Bridge / Cryptid Hotspot
**Investigated by**: BuzzFeed Unsolved, Ghost Adventures, [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], and hundreds of independent Texas paranormal societies.
## History:
- #### Construction (1884):
	- Built by the King Iron Bridge Manufacturing Co., the iron-and-wood truss bridge originally served as a vital structural crossing over Hickory Creek for horse-drawn wagons and early automobiles.
- #### The Bypassing (2001): 
	- A modern concrete vehicle bridge was constructed directly adjacent to the historic site. The Old Alton Bridge was subsequently closed to vehicular traffic and preserved strictly as a pedestrian crossing connected to local nature trails.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Physical Demonic Attacks:
	- Countless reports of hikers and investigators being physically touched, shoved, grabbed by the legs, or violently scratched. 
- #### Automated Vehicle Malfunctions: 
	- Visitors parking near the bridge approach report their car doors mysteriously locking and unlocking by themselves, headlights failing, and car horns honking without human touch.
- #### Olfactory Warnings:
	- The sudden, thick manifestation of the pungent smell of decaying flesh or sulfur drifting across the path right before a negative spiritual encounter.
- #### Visual Cryptid Anomalies: 
	- Sightings of a towering, satyr-like half-man, half-goat entity moving through the thick tree lines or standing directly on the center span.

## Entities/Spirits:
- #### [[Oscar Washburn\|Oscar Washburn]]: 
	- According to dominant local lore, he was a successful Black goat herder known as the "Goatman". Legend states he was dragged to the bridge by local Klansmen in August 1938 and thrown over a noose—only for his body to vanish mid-air before hitting the water. His spirit is said to seek revenge on the bridge.
- #### The True Demon / Hellspawn: 
	- A dark, ancient non-human entity. Demonologists argue that centuries of historical trauma, paired with alleged modern occult rituals performed under the bridge, created an aggressive elemental that merely mimics the appearance of a goat-headed man.
- #### [[Steve (KKK Leader)\|Steve (KKK Leader)]]: 
	- Believed to be the lingering, hostile spirit of a historical regional KKK Grand Dragon. Investigators note this entity behaves with extreme malice, delivering deep scratches and throwing rocks at living people who trespass.
- #### The Crying Woman:
	- The residual spirit of Washburn’s wife, rumored to wander the surrounding woods sobbing and looking for her murdered children.
## Important Events:
- #### 1938 - The Mythological Lynching: 
	- The date of the alleged attack on Oscar Washburn, permanently staining the local geography with dark legend.
- #### 2017 - The Media Boom: 
	- The synchronized release of both the Ghost Adventures and BuzzFeed Unsolved episodes catapulted the rural pedestrian bridge into an international destination for dark cryptid tourism.

## Notes:
- #### Historical Discrepancy: 
	- Local historians and researchers have uncovered zero census data, land titles, or historical news archives proving a man named Oscar Washburn ever lived in Denton County, nor is there an official record of a lynching on the bridge in 1938. The story is widely believed to be an urban legend reflecting real historical racial tensions.
- #### Occult Monitoring:
	- Local park authorities warn night visitors to stay aware of their surroundings due to verified reports of fringe groups using the secluded creek beds underneath the bridge infrastructure for animal sacrifices and occult rituals.
