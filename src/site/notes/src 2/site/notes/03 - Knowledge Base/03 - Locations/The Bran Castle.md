# Bran Castle
**Location**: Strada General Traian Moșoiu 24, Bran, Brașov County, Transylvania
**Country**: Romania
**Type**: Medieval Fortress / Royal Palace
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], independent European research groups (e.g., Amy's Crypt), and various global folklore historians.
## History:
- #### Teutonic Roots (1212): 
	- Originally established as a wooden fortification called Dietrichstein by the Teutonic Knights to guard a vital mountain pass. It was destroyed during the Mongol invasions of 1242.
- #### Saxon Fortress (1377): 
	- Louis I of Hungary granted the Saxon merchants of Brașov the right to build a permanent stone castle using their own labor and resources to defend trade routes from the Ottoman Empire.
- #### Royal Residence (1920):
	- After Transylvania became part of Greater Romania, the city of Brașov gifted the castle to Queen Marie of Romania, who heavily renovated it into a beautiful, beloved summer home.
- #### The Dracula Link: 
	- Popularized globally as the inspiration for Bram Stoker’s Dracula due to its striking, atmospheric Gothic appearance atop a jagged cliffside.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Sensory Anomalies: 
	- Visitors and staff consistently report a sudden, distinct heavy atmosphere, localized drafts, and temperature drops near the unrenovated lower stone chambers.
- #### Disembodied Audio: 
	- Faint whispers, heavy boot steps pacing empty corridors at night, and the unsettling sound of phantom children laughing echoing from the central courtyard.
- #### Apparition Sightings: 
	- Glimpses of a glowing white female figure moving gracefully through the royal chambers and outer battlements.
- #### The [[Strigoi\|Strigoi]] Presence: 
	- Local village lore insists the castle’s historic borders attract restless, blood-drinking spirits known to emerge from the surrounding mountain passes.
