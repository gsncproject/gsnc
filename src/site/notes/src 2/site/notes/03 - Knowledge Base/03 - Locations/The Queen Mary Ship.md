# RMS Queen Mary
**Location**: 1126 Queens Highway, Long Beach, California
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Retired Ocean Liner / Floating Hotel & Museum
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), Ghost Hunters (TLC/Syfy), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]] (YouTube), and various global maritime historians.
## History:
- #### The Golden Age (1936–1939):
	- Built by John Brown & Company in Scotland for the Cunard White Star Line. She sailed the North Atlantic between Southampton and New York, serving as the ultimate luxury transit for Hollywood celebrities, dignitaries, and royalty.
- #### The Grey Ghost Era (1940–1946): 
	- Stripped of her luxury fittings and painted a camouflage gray for military transport during World War II. Because of her immense speed, she routinely outran Nazi U-boats, leading Adolf Hitler to offer a massive bounty to anyone who could sink her. 
- #### The Long Beach Retirement (1967): 
	- After completing 1,001 successful transatlantic crossings, she was retired to Long Beach, California, where she was permanently docked and converted into a tourist destination, restaurant complex, and hotel.
