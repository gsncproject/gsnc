# Frank
**Type**: Spectral Apparition / Residual Spirit
**Aliases**: Frank the Phantom Bouncer, The Bordello Bodyguard

**First mentioned**: [[02 - Video GSNC/Volume I (2021-2022)/01 - July 2021 - September 2021/2021-08-01 - Shanley Hotel\|2021-08-01 - Shanley Hotel]]

## Description:
Frank is reputed to be the spirit of a Prohibition-era hitman, bodyguard, or bouncer who was employed to maintain order and protect the illegal, hidden brothel operating on the upper levels of [The Haunted Shanley Hotel](https://hauntedus.com). Unlike the dark and aggressive entities found in the building's attic space, Frank is widely celebrated in local lore for his booming, mischievous personality and dark sense of humor. He serves as an anchor spirit for overnight investigators, often interacting directly via audio devices to make guests laugh.

## Associated Events:
* **1920s (The Speakeasy Era):** Shady criminal elements and underground mafia syndicates frequented the hotel's hidden bars, defining Frank's alleged era of enforcement and security.
* **2005–Present (The Humorous Investigation Target):** Following the hotel's emergence as a premier ghost-hunting destination, Frank became an ongoing favorite for tourists due to his distinct, boisterous, and cooperative communication style.
* **2019 (The "You Liked It" Captures):** Hotel management documented high-activity Electronic Voice Phenomenon (EVP) sessions where Frank directly joked with investigators, setting his reputation as a resident comedian among the hotel's 30+ spirits.
