# Adam
**Type**: Spectral Apparition / Aggressive Ghost
**Aliases**: The Serial Killer Ghost

**First mentioned**: [[02 - Video GSNC/Volume I (2021-2022)/01 - July 2021 - September 2021/2021-08-01 - Shanley Hotel\|2021-08-01 - Shanley Hotel]]
**Locations**: [[03 - Knowledge Base/03 - Locations/The Shanley Hotel\|The Shanley Hotel]] (Napanoch, New York), Specifically "Adam's Room" located on the upper levels / attic space.
## Description:
Adam is considered by staff and investigators to be the most hostile, sinister, and threatening malevolent spirit residing within [The Haunted Shanley Hotel](https://thehauntedshanleyhotel.com/). Unlike the friendly or flirtatious entities on the second floor, Adam identifies himself through electronic equipment as a historic serial killer. He is notorious for generating a heavy, oppressive energy, and his presence is localized to a specific upper-level room where intense physical attacks have been reported.
## Associated Events:
* **Unknown Historical Era:** Rumored to have used the area or the hotel as a base or hideout during his lifetime, though his exact dates of existence are obscure. 
* **2021 (The Viral Choking Incident):** During highly publicized digital paranormal investigations (including [Sam and Colby's Shanley Hunt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MXLtFE0MiiU)), investigators documented "Adam's Room" as the location where visitors and past psychics have suffered physical choking sensations.
* **2024 (ScareNetwork Feature):** Highlighted in regional paranormal broadcasts detailing the violent history of the upper floor, solidifying his reputation as the hotel's resident negative entity.
## Statements/Claims:
* **The Body Count Claim:** When paranormal teams utilize spirit boxes and EVPs to interview the entity, it regularly speaks numbers ranging from **"17" to "38"** when asked directly how many people he murdered in life.
* **The "Leave" Commands:** The most common audio capture associated with Adam consists of a growling, deep disembodied voice demanding that investigators "Leave" or stating that he is "Behind you."
* **Psychic Threats:** Hotel staff medium Stephanie publicly documented an incident where Adam’s voice came through an SB-7 Spirit Box, explicitly threatening to "take her daughter away" because he was angered by her presence.
## Behavior/Characteristics:
* **Physical Aggression:** He is known to scratch, bruise, and physically choke hotel staff and overnight guests who refuse to leave his designated room.
* **Hostility to Solitude:** He targets individuals who isolate themselves in his territory, responding with rapid battery drainage, heavy breathing sounds, and sudden ambient temperature drops.
* **Vile Communication:** Unlike neighboring spirits, he uses severe expletives, insults, and aggressive language through modern scanning equipment.
## Connections:
* **The Irish Mafia:** Speculated by hotel mediums to have potentially been a hitman or associate linked to the underground criminal factions that frequented the hotel's illegal Prohibition-era speakeasy.

## Patterns:
* **Negative Escalation:** His haunting signature follows a distinct escalation pattern; he begins with heavy breathing sounds to intimidate, moves to direct verbal threats, and culminates in physical touch or scratching if ignored.
* **Dominant Upper-Floor Energy:** His pattern of high-intensity malice contributes to the hotel's strict rules, which include banning Ouija boards due to the risk of feeding or drawing out his aggressive energy.
## Uncertainty:

It is highly uncertain whether the spirit is a real historical serial killer or an **elemental/demonic entity mimicking a human criminal** to generate greater fear, which paranormal entities feed upon.
* **Lack of Criminal Records:** Local Napanoch historical records and police archives feature zero unsolved serial killer cases or missing person spikes matching the 17-to-38 body count claims made through the hotel's ghost-hunting equipment.
