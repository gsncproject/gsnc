# The Smurl Family
**Type**: Human Victims / Historical Witnesses
**Also Known As**: The Smurls

**First Mentioned**: [[02 - Video GSNC/Volume III (2025-2026)/02 - HellWeek 2025/Smurl House\|Smurl House]]
**Associated Locations**: [[The Smurl House\|The Smurl House]]

## Members:
- #### Jack Smurl: 
	- The family patriarch (1942–2017); a neuropsychiatric patient who underwent prior brain surgery. He was the primary target of intense physical and sexual attacks by the alleged entity.
- #### Janet Smurl: 
	- The family matriarch (1942–2026); she deeply documented the escalating timeline of threats, including instances where she was physically pulled out of bed.
- #### John and Mary Smurl: 
	- Jack’s parents, who owned and lived in the adjoining half of the double-block duplex home.
- #### Dawn and Heather Smurl: 
	- The eldest daughters, who experienced shadow figures, disembodied cries, and horrific levitation phenomena in their bedrooms.
- #### Carin and Shannon Smurl: 
	- The younger twin daughters, who were children during the height of the 1980s media circus surrounding the house.

## Connections:
- #### [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/02 - Historical People/Ed and Lorraine Warren\|Ed and Lorraine Warren]]: 
	- The prominent demonologists called to investigate the home in January 1986. They classified the active infestation as an exceptionally dangerous demonic presence.
- #### Rosemary Frueh: 
	- A registered nurse and psychic medium who accompanied the Warrens to evaluate the psychological and physical health of the family.
- #### Father Joseph Adonizio: 
	- The local parish priest from Immaculate Conception Church who provided pastoral support and blessings to the family.

## Notes:
- #### The Haunting Profile: 
	- Active from 1974 to 1989, the case involves terrifying phenomena such as horrific foul odors, levitating furniture, loud pounding in threes, their 75-pound German Shepherd being hurled against walls, and targeted sexual battery.
- #### Media Phenomenon:
	- The family's plight triggered a massive national media frenzy in 1986, culminating in a book titled The Haunted, a subsequent 1991 made-for-TV movie, and served as the direct inspiration for the cinematic film The Conjuring: Last Rites.
- #### The "Four Entity" Theory: 
	- According to the Warrens, the home was not possessed by one spirit, but rather by an old woman, a violent young girl, a deceased man, and a powerful, localized demon manipulating them all.
- #### Skeptical Backlash: 
	- The case remains deeply controversial. Prominent skeptics such as Paul Kurtz attributed the events to mass hysteria and Jack's underlying neurological health issues. Subsequent tenants who moved into the duplex after the Smurls departed in 1988 reported zero supernatural activity.
