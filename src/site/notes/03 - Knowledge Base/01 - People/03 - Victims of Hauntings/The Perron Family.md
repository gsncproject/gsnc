# The Perron Family
**Type**: Human Victims / Historical Witnesses
**Also Known As**: The Perrons

**First Mentioned**: [[02 - Video GSNC/Volume I (2021-2022)/01 - July 2021 - September 2021/2021-07-25 - Conjuring House\|2021-07-25 - Conjuring House]]
**Associated Locations**: [[03 - Knowledge Base/03 - Locations/The Conjuring House\|The Conjuring House]]

## Members:
- #### Roger Perron: 
	- The family patriarch; a hard-working blue-collar father who was deeply skeptical of the paranormal but fiercely protective of his family.
- #### Carolyn Perron: 
	- The family matriarch; she bore the brunt of the spiritual attacks, experiencing severe psychological torment, physical bruising, and eventual possession.
- #### Andrea Perron: 
	- The eldest daughter and author who later documented the family's decade-long ordeal in her biographical trilogy.
- #### Nancy Perron: 
	- The second eldest daughter; often remembered for interacting with a localized childhood spirit on the property.
- #### Christine Perron: 
	- The middle child; notably reported having her leg pulled in the middle of the night, a scene later adapted into cinema.
- #### Cindy Perron: 
	- The fourth daughter; reported hearing disembodied whispers in her bedroom and finding secret spaces in the house.
- #### April Perron: 
	- The youngest daughter; allegedly befriended a benign spirit named "[[Rory\|Rory]]" who manifested as a young boy.

## Connections:
- #### [[03 - Knowledge Base/03 - Locations/The Conjuring House\|The Conjuring House]]: 
	- Their primary residence from December 1970 to 1980, where they experienced a decade of intense hauntings.
- #### [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/02 - Historical People/Bathsheeba Sherman\|Bathsheeba Sherman]]: 
	- The negative entity identified by investigators as the primary tormentor of the household, specifically targeting Carolyn.
- #### [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/02 - Historical People/Ed and Lorraine Warren\|Ed and Lorraine Warren]]: 
	- The paranormal investigators called in to help the family, though their intervention ultimately escalated the activity.

## Notes:
- #### Duration of Stay: 
	- Despite the horrific events, financial constraints forced the family to live in the haunted farmhouse for ten full years before they could afford to relocate to Georgia in 1880.
- #### Literary Documentation: 
	- Andrea Perron wrote the three-volume memoir series House of Darkness House of Light, which serves as the primary primary-source account of the family's experiences.
- #### The Séance Trauma: 
	- The family remains split on the legacy of the Warrens; while they acknowledge the investigators' intent, the botched 1974 séance left Carolyn severely traumatized and resulted in Roger kicking the Warrens off the property.
