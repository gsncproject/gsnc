# The Cosmopolitan Hotel
**Location**: 2660 Calhoun Street, Old Town San Diego, California
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Hotel, Former Adobe Estate
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures Crew (Travel Channel), independent digital paranormal creators (e.g., [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/01 - Collaborations/FazeRug\|FazeRug]]), and regional ghost tour operators.
## History:
- #### La Casa de Bandini (1827–1829): 
	- Originally constructed as a massive, one-story Spanish-Colonial u-shaped adobe mansion by [[Don Juan Bandini\|Don Juan Bandini]]. It stood as one of the largest single-family homes in early San Diego, hosting legendary multi-day parties and political gatherings.
- #### Seeley’s Transformation (1869): 
	- Purchased by [[Albert Seeley\|Albert Seeley]], who added a second-story wooden frame, a wrapping grand Victorian balcony, and repurposed the property as a luxury stagecoach stop and inn.
- #### Adaptive Reuse: 
	- Served as an olive cannery around 1900, reverted to a high-end restaurant in the mid-to-late 20th century, and underwent a massive 2010 historical restoration to revive its 1870s stagecoach charm.
