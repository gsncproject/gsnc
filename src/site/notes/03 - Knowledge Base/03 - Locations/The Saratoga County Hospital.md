# Saratoga County Homestead
**Location**: 7239 Barkersville Road, Middle Grove (Providence Township), New York
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Abandoned Tuberculosis Sanatorium / Infirmary
**Investigated by**: Destination Fear (Travel Channel), [[Project Fear\|Project Fear]] (YouTube), independent research teams (e.g., [[Haunted Nights\|Haunted Nights]]), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]] and regional urban explorers.
## History:
- #### The Fresh Air Era (1914):
	- Opened as a secluded mountain sanatorium to care for patients suffering from the tuberculosis epidemic. It was intentionally sited at an elevation of 1,400 feet for its clean, cool Adirondack air, which was believed to be vital for therapeutic lung recovery.
- #### The Brick Expansion (1932–1936): 
	- The original wooden structures were replaced by a massive, 31,000-square-foot Georgian-style brick facility equipped with columns, marble molding, and expansive solariums designed to constantly circulate fresh air.
- #### Geriatric Transition (1961):
	- After advanced antibiotics like streptomycin rendered dedicated TB hospitals obsolete, the site reopened as the Saratoga County Infirmary, providing nursing care to elderly patients.
- #### Shuttering and Decay (1979): 
	- The facility closed its doors permanently. Left abandoned for nearly half a century, it suffered devastating vandalism, structural collapses, and significant fire damage in 2014.
