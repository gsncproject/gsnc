# Indiana State Sanatorium
**Location**: 3838 East Old 36 Road, Rockville, Parke County, Indiana
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Abandoned Tuberculosis Sanatorium / Former Mental Hospital & Nursing Home
**Investigated by**: Destination Fear / [[Project Fear\|Project Fear]] (Travel Channel/YouTube), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], and numerous independent midwestern paranormal societies.
## History:
- #### The Fight Against the White Plague (1907–1911): 
	- Authorized by the Indiana General Assembly in 1907 to combat the deadly tuberculosis epidemic. The complex officially opened to infected patients in 1911 as a remote, self-contained quarantine camp.
- #### Self-Sustaining Fortress: 
	- Spanning over 200 acres, the campus operated as its own independent city, housing staff dormitories, an extensive greenhouse, a dairy barn, a bakery, a water tower, and its own operational coal mine connected by a dedicated bridge.
- #### The Psychiatric Transition (1976–2011): 
	- Following the invention of anti-tuberculosis antibiotics, the TB center shut down in 1968. It was repurposed and reopened in 1976 as the Lee Alan Bryant Health Care Center, operating as a private mental hospital and geriatric nursing facility. 
- #### The Panic Closure (2011): 
	- Facing a barrage of structural issues, state lawsuits, and severe patient neglect allegations, the facility was abruptly closed and chained shut overnight in 2011, leaving hundreds of psychiatric beds, medical files, and personal suitcases scattered inside.
