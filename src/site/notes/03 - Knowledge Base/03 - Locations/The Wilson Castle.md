# Wilson Castle
**Location**: 2970 West Proctor Road, Proctor, Vermont
**Country**: United States
**Type**: 19th-Century Estate / Victorian Castle
**Investigated by**: Jason Hawes (Ghost Hunters), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], [[Haunted Nights\|Haunted Nights]], Sinisterhood, and local teams like Dark Knights Paranormal.
## History:
- #### Johnson's Folly (1885–1887): 
	- Construction began under Dr. [[John Johnson\|John Johnson]] and his wealthy English wife, [[Sara Johnson\|Sara Johnson]]. Built over a multi-year period at a staggering cost of $1.3 million, the 13,000-square-foot architectural marvel combined Scottish Baronial and Queen Anne styles. 
- #### Financial Ruin: 
	- The Johnsons lived in the estate for only a brief period before massive financial strain forced them to abandon the property, leading to the liquidation of its priceless European antiques.
- #### The Wilson Dynasty (1939–2009): 
	- Purchased by Colonel [[Herbert Wilson\|Herbert Wilson]] for a meager $12,000. The Wilson family occupied and maintained the massive 32-room estate through three successive generations.
- #### Modern Tourist Era: 
	- Currently owned by Colonel Wilson’s granddaughter, [[Denise Davine\|Denise Davine]]. The home operates dynamically as a historic house museum by day and a highly booked hotspot for commercial ghost tours and overnight lockdowns by night.
