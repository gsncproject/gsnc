# The Stanley Hotel
**Location**: 333 East Wonderview Avenue, Estes Park, Colorado
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Mountain Resort / Active Paranormal Hotel
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), Ghost Hunters (Syfy/A&E), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]] (YouTube), and thousands of independent researchers worldwide. 
## History:
- #### The Tuberculous Escape (1903–1909): 
	- [[F.O Stanley\|Freelan Oscar Stanley]], co-inventor of the famous Stanley Steamer automobile, moved to the dry, sun-drenched air of Estes Park after being diagnosed with severe tuberculosis. His health rapidly recovered, prompting him to build a grand resort.
- #### Edwardian Opulence (1909): 
	- The palatial, 48-room Colonial Revival hotel officially opened, bringing East Coast luxury, electricity, and modern plumbing to the rugged Colorado wilderness.
- #### The Stephen King Catalyst (1974): 
	- After decades of structural neglect and declining tourism, horror author Stephen King stayed in Room 217 on the final night of the hotel's seasonal operations. The empty, snowbound atmosphere inspired his bestselling masterpiece, The Shining.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Phantom Piano Melodies: 
	- Sound signatures of classical piano music drifting from the empty MacGregor Ballroom late at night. 
- #### The Fourth-Floor Playroom: 
	- Intense, recurring sounds of invisible children laughing, giggling, and running down the halls on the uppermost level.
- #### Poltergeist Tidying: 
	- Inside Room 217, guests routinely report finding their open luggage completely unpacked, clothes folded neatly, or items shifted by an unseen entity.
- #### Apparition Sightings: 
	- Visual reports of a formal Edwardian man (believed to be F.O. Stanley) standing in the lobby or watching guests from the staircase.

## Important Events:
- #### June 25, 1911 - The Gas Explosion: 
	- A severe lightning storm knocked out electricity, forcing staff to light emergency gas lamps. The accumulation of gas triggered an explosion that blew Elizabeth Wilson through the floorboard into the dining room below, fundamentally shifting the hotel's haunting timeline.
- #### October 30, 1974 - The Nightmare: 
	- Stephen King woke up in a cold sweat in Room 217 after dreaming of his young son being chased through the empty corridors by a rogue fire hose, cementing the layout of the fictional Overlook Hotel

## Notes:
- #### The Kubrick Disconnect: 
	- While the hotel inspired Stephen King's original text, filmmaker Stanley Kubrick chose to utilize the exterior layout of the Timberline Lodge in Oregon and constructed internal sets for his 1980 film adaptation—a creative choice King openly disliked.
