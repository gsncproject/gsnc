# St. Augustine Lighthouse
**Location**: 100 Red Cox Drive, St. Augustine, Florida
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Active Maritime Beacon / Museum
**Investigated by**: TAPS / *Ghost Hunters* (Syfy/A&E), *[[The Boys\|The Boys]]* (YouTube), and [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]]
## History:
- #### The First Beacons (1500s–1824): 
	- Spanish watchtowers stood on the site for centuries to safeguard the nation's oldest city. The original wooden framework was officially designated as Florida’s first official lighthouse in 1824.
- #### The Modern Tower (1871–1874): 
	- Due to severe shoreline erosion threatening the old structure, construction began on the current 165-foot brick-and-mortar tower. It was officially lit for operation in October 1874.
- #### Automation and Preservation:
	- The light station was completely automated by the U.S. Coast Guard in 1971. Following an arson attempt in 1970, the Junior Service League of St. Augustine restored the Keeper's House and established the current [St. Augustine Lighthouse & Maritime Museum](https://www.staugustinelighthouse.org/).
## Alleged Activity:
- #### The Catwalk Apparition: 
	- Repeated sightings of a pale female figure looking down directly from the metal outer observation railing.
- #### Auditory Echoes: 
	- The distinct sound of footsteps running up and down the 219 metal spiral steps when the tower is entirely locked down.
- #### Phantom Cigar Smoke: 
	- Dense wafts of premium tobacco scent manifesting in the strictly smoke-free Keeper's Quarters and tower landings.
- #### Childish Giggles: 
	- Sound signatures of young children playing, giggling, and running through the dark, wooded paths surrounding the grounds.

## Entities/Spirits:
- ### The Pittee Sisters ([[Eliza Pittee\|Eliza Pittee]] and [[Mary Pittee\|Mary Pittee]]): 
	- In July 1873, the young daughters of construction superintendent Hezekiah Pittee tragically drowned alongside another young African-American girl when their transport cart slipped into the bay. They are the most famous spirits reported on-site.
- #### [[Joseph Andreu\|Joseph Andreu]]:
	- A dedicated mid-19th-century keeper who tragically fell to his death in 1859 while painting the exterior scaffolding of the old tower. His spirit is traditionally tied to the phantom cigar aromas.
- #### [[Major William Harn\|William A. Harn]]: 
	- A decorated Civil War veteran and former lighthouse keeper who passed away on the property from tuberculosis. He is primarily spotted walking the dark basements.
## Important Events:
- #### July 10, 1873 - The Construction Tragedy: 
	- The fatal launch-cart accident that claimed the lives of the Pittee sisters, fundamentally anchoring the site's tragic lore.
- #### 2006 - The National Broadcast: 
	- The premiere of the Ghost Hunters episode catapulted the regional museum into an international dark tourism destination.
## Recurring Elements:
- #### "Raining in the Tower":
	- A rare, distinct atmospheric phenomenon occurring during deep winter where hot air hits freezing iron landings, creating indoor condensation drops. Skeptics note this micro-climate shift can warp thermal readings or mimic phantom drips.
- #### Poltergeist Pranks: 
	- Relocating gift shop keys, untying staff member shoes, and setting off movement alarms in the dead of night.

- #### Operational Tourism:
