### History:
- The story of the first Conjuring Movie originates from that house. Meaning that almost everything that happened in the movie happened in real life too. 
- The Perron Family describes the house as a portal disguised as a farm house.
- Bathsheba Sherman, who is the witch in the Conjuring Movies was actually a real person but it was never known if she really was a witch or just some cranky weird lady.
- During the tour Amanda sees children and Cory confirms that children are seen around the house.
- As they went into the basement, Cory heard something saying “Hey Sam”, and Kat felt like Stas touched her but no one touched her.
- Outside Amanda sees three men, probably soldiers, walking towards the house.
