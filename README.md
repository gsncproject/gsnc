# GSNC-Guide-for-Sam-and-Colby

## What is the GSNC?
The GSNC is a long term project that began back in 2022 with my fourteen year old self, and one of my then best friends. I have always been interested in the paranormal but neither denied nor agreed to the existance of ghosts and alike (even today). The original GSNC started because two fourteen year old girls loved to hunt ghosts and because they had a notebook and pen and way too much free time and imagination. I can assure you that, due to us being so young, we didn't understand everything neither did we always made the right choices when it came to the paranormal; because on my fifteenth birthday we tried summoning Lillith. But beside taking risks I would slap myself for nowadays, I started documenting whenever I found something unexplanable but interesting in Sam and Colby's videos, and the more videos I watched the more the GSNC filled with their instead of our investigations.
## What is in the GSNC?
As already stated it consists of Sam and Colby's videos and them being linked together, but before it was that it was just a chaos that you couldn't even start to try and clean up, which is why I began to write down everything on Obsidian to keep it organized and better to navigate for me and whoever else will read this giant ass archive.
## What is the GSNC for and what is the goal?
The GSNC is for those who know that Sam and Colby's videos are intertwined but also for those who thought about doing something like this archive themselves (but because its too much work, I suffer for everyone :D), whilst the original goal of this archive was to print it into books and give it to Sam and Colby if they ever found out about its existance and would be interested in reading it. Now, printing books is pretty expensive so I found a cheaper way (actually free way) to make it accessable for everyone who would like to peek inside.

Other than that; I am always happy when people read it and find things that I might've missed during the first twenty go-throughs. I also do love hearing other people's thoughts and theories on Sam and Colby's videos and whatsoever.

That being said, have fun reading and looking through it!
