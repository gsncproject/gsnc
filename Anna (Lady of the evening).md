# Anna
**Type**: Spectral Apparition / Alleged Ghost
**Aliases**: None widely known (The Bordello Madam/Worker)

**First mentioned**: [[2021-08-01 - Shanley Hotel]]
**Locations**: [[The Shanley Hotel]] (Napanoch, New York), Specifically "Anna's Room" located in the second-floor former bordello area.

## Description:
Anna is believed to be the spirit of a 1920s woman who worked alongside Maddie in the illegal, hidden brothel operating on the upper floors of [The Haunted Shanley Hotel](https://thehauntedshanleyhotel.com/haunted-rooms/) during the Prohibition era. Like her contemporary Maddie, Anna is celebrated in regional ghost lore as a permanent resident of the hotel. Overnight guests who book "Anna's Room" describe her spirit as distinctly present, watchful, and highly interactive.

## Associated Events:
* **1920s (The Secret Brothel Era):** A quiet, underground network of bootlegging and prostitution operated in the hotel's upper wing, defining Anna's alleged earthly environment.
* **2005–Present (Overnight Ghost Tourism):** Following the public conversion of the hotel into a dedicated paranormal destination, Anna’s room was preserved as a primary hub for electronic voice phenomenon (EVP) research.
* **2015 (The HorrorBuzz Investigation):** A prominent media group made verifiable contact with a spirit identifying as Anna, using flashlight and EVP techniques to capture direct conversational audio.

## Statements/Claims:
* **The "I Love You" Claim:** Investigators tracking paranormal phenomena documented an audio capture via an EVP recorder where a female voice softly but clearly whispered, "I love you," which was attributed directly to Anna's spirit.
* **The Facial Hair Preference:** Hotel guides and seasoned investigators state that while Maddie targets younger men, Anna’s spirit displays a strong, specific preference for interacting with male visitors who have facial hair.
* **The Guest Log Claims:** Logbooks left inside her room feature guest accounts detailing the feeling of being actively watched by a figure looming near the bed, alongside unexplained dawn "scratching" at the windows.

## Behavior/Characteristics:
* **Selective Flirtation:** Known for being highly responsive to male energy, particularly men matching her physical preferences. 
* **The "Pennies From Heaven" Phenomenon:** Guests sleeping in her room frequently report discovering loose pennies appearing out of nowhere on the nightstand, floor, or bed linens.
* **Apparatus Responsiveness:** Proves highly cooperative during structured "Yes/No" isolation tests, utilizing specialized gear like REM pods, EM pumps, and the Estes Method to communicate.

## Connections:
* **Maddie:** Her spectral neighbor and historical coworker in the hidden 1920s bordello wing.
* **Beatrice and James Shanley:** The turn-of-the-century proprietors who ran the legitimate fronts of the hotel.
* **Rosie:** The spirit of the 3-year-old child who passed away on the property and operates nearby.

## Patterns:
* **Bordello Multi-Manifestations:** Her behavior follows a repeating localized pattern where multiple spirits from the Prohibition era feed off each other's residual energy, escalating activity when guests try to interact with both her and Maddie simultaneously.
* **Affectionate Tones:** Unlike the more aggressive or melancholic spirits residing on the third floor, Anna's interactions fall into a pattern of friendliness, warmth, and gentleness toward living occupants.

## Uncertainty:
* **Historical Verification:** Just as with Maddie, there are no legal employment records, ledger sheets, or census tracking items that prove a woman named Anna lived or died inside the establishment. Her identity relies completely on names provided via spirit box sessions.
* **Audio Pareidolia:** Skeptics argue that because "Anna" is a short, common name with simple phonetic vowel sounds, investigators utilizing sweeping radio frequencies (Spirit Boxes) are prone to mishearing basic static or white noise as her name.
