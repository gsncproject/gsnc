# Bathsheeba Sherman 
**Type**: Real Person 
**Aliases**: Conjuring Witch
 
**First mentioned**: [[02 - Video GSNC/Volume I (2021-2022)/01 - July 2021 - September 2021/2021-07-25 - Conjuring House\|2021-07-25 - Conjuring House]]
**Locations**: [[03 - Knowledge Base/03 - Locations/The Conjuring House\|The Conjuring House]]

## Description: 
- #### Historical Reality:
	- A 19th-Century Rhode Island Housewife and resident. Born Bathsheeba Thayer in 1812, she married farmer Judson Sherman in 1844 and lived an ordinary agrarian life.
- #### Conjuring Universe/Lore:
	- Depicted in a local folklore and cinematic adaptations as a grotesque, gaunt, twisted spectral entity. Pop culture protrays her with a haggaard face and tattered clothes, embodying a malicious poltergeist.
## Associated Events:
- #### The 1840s/1860s Infant Death Trial:
	- A local infant died while in her care, showing signs of a head wound. Although she was tried and aquitted of murder due to lack of evidence, this event triggered decades of local folklore.
- #### [[The Harrisville Farmhouse Haunting (1971-1980)\|The Harrisville Farmhouse Haunting (1971-1980)]]:
	- The [[Perron Family\|Perron Family]] moved into the old Arnold Estate and experienced a lot of paranormal appearances. Demonologists [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/02 - Historical People/Ed and Lorraine Warren\|Ed and Lorraine Warren]] credited Bathsheeba with the core hauntings.
## Statements/Claims:
- #### Folklore Accusations:
	- Local legends claimed she was a devout Satanist, who sacrificed her new born baby (or an infant in her care) to the Devil using a sewing needle
- #### The Deathbed Curse:
	- Lore says that before committing suicide, she climbed a tree on the property, swore her devotion to Satan, and cursed anyone who dared take or occupy her land.
- #### Cinematic Retelling:
	- The 2013 Conjuring Movie claims she was related to [[Mary Towne Eastey\|Mary Towne Eastey]], a woman executed during the 1692 Salam Witch Trials
## Behavior/Characteristics:
- #### Historical Records:
	- Described as a normal housewife, mother to a son named Herbert and a member of the local community who died of a stroke/paralysis in 1885.
- #### Paranormal Lore Entity:
	- Described as highly aggressive, territorial, and deeply malevolent. Alleged behaviors include pinning down individuals, causing unexplained puncture wounds, whispering threats to “get out“, and attempting to possess mothers to harm their children.

## Patterns:
- #### Targeting Mothers:
	- Both in lore and cinematic media, her entity targets the martriach of the household (i.e Carolyn Perron) in an attempt to break her psychological state and force child harm.
- #### Territorial Escalation:
	- Folklore outlines a pattern where the haunting escalates from vanishing household items and cold drafts to physical, violent assaults when new families attempt to establish ownership of the land.
## Uncertainty:
- #### False Suicide Narrative:
	- Local folklore and film narratives claim that she hanged herself on the Perron property. Historical records completely debunk this.
