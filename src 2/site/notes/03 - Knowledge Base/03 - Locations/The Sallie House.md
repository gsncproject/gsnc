# The Sallie House
**Location**: 508 North 20th Street, Atchison, Kansas
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Residential Home
**Investigated by**: Sightings (Fox), Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), BuzzFeed Unsolved, Watcher (Ghost Files), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]] and numerous independent demonologists.
## History:
- #### Construction (1867–1872):
	- Built as a modest family home in Atchison, Kansas. It was occupied for generations, notably by the Finney family, where a family member used the front room as a localized medical practice.
- #### The Pickman Era (1992–1994):
	- Renters [[Tony Pickman\|Tony Pickman]] and [[Debra Pickman\|Debra Pickman]] moved into the home with their newborn son. They experienced an immediate, violent escalation of paranormal phenomena, forcing them to flee the property after two years.
- #### Modern Status: 
	- Owned by a local preservationist and left empty of permanent residents, the home is operated entirely as a research site for overnight paranormal investigations.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Violent Scratches: 
	- The house is infamous for physical assaults, primarily targeting adult men (most notably Tony Pickman) with deep, bleeding scratch marks, burns, and welts that manifest instantly.
- #### Spontaneous Fires: 
	- Unexplained small fires igniting inside the home without an ignition source, including candles lighting themselves or small piles of clothing combusting.
- #### Apparitions: 
	- Visual sightings of a small girl floating in rooms, as well as a middle-aged woman dressed in early 20th-century attire.
- #### Animal Terror: 
	- Domestic dogs brought to the property routinely refuse to enter the nursery room, barking hysterically at empty corners before retreating.
## Investigations:
- [[02 - Video GSNC/Volume I (2021-2022)/03 - November 2021 - December 2021/2021-12-19 - Sallie House\|2021-12-19 - Sallie House]]
- #### 1993 - The Sightings Broadcast: 
	- The television show Sightings documented the Pickman family over several months. Cameras captured live EMF spikes, moving objects, and physical scratch marks appearing on Tony Pickman during filming.
- #### Ghost Adventures Lock-down:
	- Zak Bagans and his team documented extreme spatial temperature drops, localized battery drains, and captured a child's voice on electronic equipment in the nursery.
- #### Watcher (Ghost Files): 
	- Ryan Bergara and Shane Madej tested the property's reputation using extensive environmental monitors, mapping unexplained interactive anomalies in the basement.
## Entities/Spirits:
- #### [[Sallie Lucy\|Sallie]]: 
	- The alleged spirit of a 6-year-old girl. According to lore, she died on the operating table in the house during an emergency appendectomy performed without anesthesia by Dr. Charles Finney.
- #### The Dark Matrix / The Demon:
	- A deeply aggressive, malevolent non-human presence. Investigators believe it mimics the identity of a little girl ("Sallie") to lower victims' defenses before executing psychological and physical attacks.
- #### The Grumpy Woman: 
	- A lingering female spirit believed to be a protective relative of the original household, often associated with a heavy, watchful feeling in the kitchen area.
## Important Events:
- #### 1900s - The Tragic Operation: 
	- The rumored historical death of the young girl Sallie, anchoring the home's permanent dark folklore.
- #### 1993 - The Pickman Attack: 
	- Tony Pickman was physically attacked and scratched in mid-air during a recorded investigation, cementing the home's reputation as one of the most violent locations in America.
## Recurring Elements:
- #### Targeting of Adult Men: 
	- The negative energy in the house displays a distinct pattern of hostility toward adult male investigators, while remaining relatively calm around female occupants.
- #### The Nursery Center: 
	- The upstairs nursery room stands as the absolute epicenter for kinetic toy movement, cold spots, and sudden electronic distortion.
- #### The Basement Hatch:
	- The dark basement area is widely documented as the focal point for the home's heavier, demonic energy signatures.
