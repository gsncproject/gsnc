# Yorktown Memorial Hospital
**Location**: 728 West Main Street, Yorktown, DeWitt County, Texas
Country: United States
**Type**: Abandoned Rehabilitation Hospital / Convent
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), Destination Fear, BuzzFeed Unsolved, Watcher (Ghost Files), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], and numerous global paranormal teams.
## History:
- #### The Felician Sisters (1950–1951):
	- Built and operated by the Felician Sisters, a prominent Roman Catholic religious order of nuns, along with local community funding, to serve as a high-capacity modern hospital for the regional Texas population.
- #### The Rehabilitation Shift (1986): 
	- Due to severe financial strain and shifting municipal needs, the hospital closed its emergency doors and was repurposed as a specialized drug and alcohol rehabilitation center.
- #### Absolute Shuttering (1992): 
	- Following multiple operational complications, the facility was completely abandoned. It sat empty and decaying under the Texas sun for decades before being purchased by a private owner for preservation and dark tourism.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Glowing Red Eyes: 
	- Infamous sightings of a pair of disembodied, glowing red eyes staring down investigators from the end of the dark, main hospital corridors.
- #### Aggressive [[Shadow Figures\|Shadow Figures]]:
	- Large, imposing dark silhouettes that physically block door frames and run rapidly across the walls of the old chapel.
- #### Auditory Religious Echoes:
	- The sound of disembodied chanting, phantom organ music, and nuns whispering Latin prayers echoing through the vacant building at night.
- #### Kinetic Attacks:
	- Visitors reporting being physically scratched, pushed, or having small medical objects thrown at them when exploring the operating rooms.
## Investigations:
- #### Ghost Adventures Lockdown:
	- Zak Bagans and the crew documented extreme electromagnetic field (EMF) spikes, recorded intelligent electronic voice phenomena (EVPs) responding in Polish, and monitored shadows moving independently of light sources.
- ### Watcher (Ghost Files): 
	- Ryan Bergara and Shane Madej heavily monitored the old chapel and patient bays with spatial tracking tech, mapping unexplained interactive anomalies across the floor plan.
## Entities/Spirits:
- #### The Talking Doll ([[Jeannie (the talking doll)\|Jeannie (the talking doll)]]): 
	- A vintage doll left as a trigger object in the nursery. Investigators report the doll's voice box activating dynamically without mechanical intervention, answering targeted questions.
- #### The Strict Nuns: 
	- Multiple residual spirits of the Felician Sisters who continue to patrol the halls, known for displaying an authoritative, deeply religious demeanor toward investigators.
- #### The Boiler Room Entity: 
	- A highly hostile, non-human malevolent presence that resides in the subterranean boiler room and basement, known to cause intense feelings of dread and nausea.
- #### [[Dr. Nowierski\|Dr. Nowierski]]: 
	- A rumored historical physician whose phantom footsteps and heavy energy are said to still linger around the main surgical suites.
## Important Events:
- #### 1951 - The Official Dedication: 
	- The massive brick medical center is officially blessed by Catholic clergy, beginning forty years of intensive, high-casualty medical operations.
- #### 1992 - The Quiet Departure: 
	- The facility falls completely silent, leaving behind a vast time capsule of vintage medical equipment, operational charts, and religious iconography.
## Recurring Elements:
- #### The "No Swearing" Rule: 
	- The entities of the nuns are highly sensitive to profanity. Swearing routinely triggers immediate paranormal responses, including flashing K-II meters, loud slamming doors, or physical scratch marks.
- #### The Triple-Knock Phenomenon: 
	- The sound of distinct, loud pounding in sequences of three echoing from inside locked patient rooms, traditionally interpreted by demonologists as a mockery of the Holy Trinity.
