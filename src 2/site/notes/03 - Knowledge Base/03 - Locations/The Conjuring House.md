# The Conjuring House
**Location**: 1677 Round Top Road, Harrisville, Rhode Island
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Farmhouse / Haunted Location
**Investigated by**: [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/02 - Historical People/Ed and Lorraine Warren\|Ed and Lorraine Warren]], [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]], [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/01 - Collaborations/ExploringWithJosh\|ExploringWithJosh]], [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/01 - Collaborations/Seth Borden\|Seth Borden]], 

## History:
- #### Construction:
	- Originally built in 1736 by the Arnold family as a multi-acre homestead and working farm in rural Rhode Island.
- #### The Perron Ownership (1970–1980): 
	- Purchased by Roger and Carolyn Perron in December 1970. The family of seven occupied the home for a decade, experiencing escalating paranormal disturbances.
- #### Commercial Era:
	- Purchased in 2019 by Cory and Jennifer Heinzen, who opened the house for paranormal tourism, media broadcasts, and overnight investigations. It was sold again in 2022 to Jacqueline Nuñez for $1.52 million.

## Alleged Activity:
- #### Apparitions: 
	- Sightings of multiple spirits, ranging from a benign, motherly presence sweeping the kitchen to a towering, aggressive malevolent entity.
- #### Auditory Phenomena: 
	- Heavy, disembodied footsteps running through the halls, doors slamming shut, and disembodied voices whispering, "There are ghosts in the walls."
- #### Physical Assaults:
	- Inhabitants being levitated in chairs, physically pushed, slapped, or pinned down in their beds by unseen forces.
- #### Olfactory Disturbances: 
	- Sudden, overpowering smells of rotting flesh or sulfur manifesting in specific rooms without a physical source.

## Investigations:
- #### The Warren Investigation (1973–1974): 
	- Renowned demonologists Ed and Lorraine Warren conducted multiple visits. They concluded the home was infested with demonic presence rather than a typical human haunting.
- #### The Séance (1974): 
	- A highly controversial and volatile séance conducted by the Warrens in the home's living room, during which Carolyn Perron allegedly spoke in tongues and levitated in her chair.
- #### Modern Media Investigations: 
	- Extensively monitored by modern paranormal groups utilizing specialized equipment (e.g., ITC devices, thermal imaging, and vibration sensors), which document ongoing unexplained anomalies.

## Entities/Spirits:
- #### [[03 - Knowledge Base/01 - People/02 - Historical People/Bathsheeba Sherman\|Bathsheeba Sherman]]: 
	- Legally a 19th-century local resident; historically branded by local lore and the Warrens as the primary, highly malicious demonic spirit ruling the land.
- #### The Arnold Family Spirits: 
	- Multiple generations of the property's original owners who are said to peacefully linger in their old living spaces.
- #### The "Seven Dead Soldiers": 
	- Lore from the Perron children regarding spirits of soldiers who allegedly died or were buried on the surrounding property grounds.

## Important Events:
- #### 1970 - The Arrival: 
	- The Perron family moves in and immediately notices anomalous activity, starting with small misplaced items and progressing to dark figures.
- #### 1974 - The Warren Séance: 
	- The climax of the early investigations, which resulted in Roger Perron ordering the Warrens to leave the property permanently out of fear for his wife's safety.
- #### 2013 - The Cinematic Release: 
	- Global release of *The Conjuring*, catapulting the private residence into mainstream international pop-culture fame and creating a hub for dark tourism.

## Recurring Elements:
- #### The Smell of Rot: 
	- A constant precursor to highly aggressive or negative paranormal occurrences on the property.
- #### Targeting of the Matriarch: 
	- A distinct trend where the dominant female presence of the house (historically Carolyn Perron) absorbs the brunt of physical and psychological trauma.
- #### 3:07 AM Anomalies: 
	- A recurring time window noted by investigators and residents where activity spikes, famously referenced in cinematic adaptations as the "witching hour."
