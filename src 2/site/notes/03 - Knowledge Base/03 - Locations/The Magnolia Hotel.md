# The Magnolia Hotel
**Location**: 203 South Crockett Street, Seguin, Texas
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Frontier Hotel / Bed & Breakfast
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), Ghost Brothers (TLC), Watcher (YouTube), [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]] and Strange Town.
## History:
- #### The Log Cabin Frontier (1840): 
	- Originally built as a simple two-room log cabin by James Campbell, a founding father of Seguin and a renowned original Texas Ranger. 
- #### Stagecoach Expansion (1848–1880): 
	- Converted and expanded into a massive 10-room hotel, tavern, and restaurant by Jeremiah Strother Calvert and subsequent owners, serving as a primary stagecoach social hub.
- #### Decay and Restoration (2012–2013): 
	- After spending decades split into small apartments, the building fell into major disrepair and was placed on Texas's "Most Endangered Historic Places" list. It was purchased and completely restored by Jim and Erin Ghedi in 2013.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Kinetic Manifestations: 
	- Photographed and filmed occurrences of coins rolling in complete circles on tables, heavy doors slamming shut, and disconnected antique telephones ringing.
- #### Shadow Entities: 
	- Visual sightings of shadow figures slipping through the narrow second-story hallways.
- #### Auditory Echoes:
	- Disembodied voices calling out the names of owners or investigators in the dead of night.
- #### Apparition Windows: 
	- Passersby routinely reporting the visage of an old woman staring down at them from the upper glass window panes.

## Important Events:
- #### 1847 - The Texas Ranger Wedding: 
	- Famous Texas Ranger Captain John "Jack" Coffee Hays married the owner's daughter, Susan Calvert, right inside the property's historic fireplace parlor.
- #### 1874 - The Faust Connection:
	- The hotel became permanently stained in regional true-crime lore when guest Wilhelm Faust used his room as a staging point for a horrific local murder spree.
- #### 2017 - The Paranormal Reopening: 
	- The Ghedis officially opened the fully restored lower levels to overnight Airbnb paranormal tourism, maintaining a near-constant booking rate.
