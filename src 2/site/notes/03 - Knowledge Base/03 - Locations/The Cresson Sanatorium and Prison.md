# Cresson Sanatorium and Prison
**Location**: 251 Correction Road, Lilly / Cresson, Cambria County, Pennsylvania
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Abandoned Tuberculosis Sanatorium / Former Mental Hospital & State Prison
**Investigated by**: Amy's Crypt, Unfortunate Travels, [[Sam and Colby\|Sam and Colby]] and numerous East Coast paranormal research societies.
## History:
- #### The Tuberculosis Era (1911–1913): 
	- Constructed on mountain land donated by steel tycoon and philanthropist [[Andrew Carnegie\|Andrew Carnegie]]. Opened in 1913, the high-altitude Allegheny Mountain air was believed to be a vital therapeutic vector for patients battling the "White Plague". 
- #### The Psychiatric Transition (1956–1982): 
	- As antibiotics cured TB, the campus was incorporated into the Lawrence F. Flick State Hospital under the Department of Public Welfare, functioning as a long-term asylum for the mentally ill.
- #### State Correctional Institution (1983–2013): 
	- Converted via a $20.6 million overhaul into SCI Cresson, a medium-security state prison housing a massive male inmate population. 
- #### The 2013 Closure: 
	- Officially decommissioned by the state due to astronomical maintenance costs and structural antiquity. The sprawling 400-acre mega-complex sat abandoned before being purchased by a private organization for historical preservation and dark tourism.
