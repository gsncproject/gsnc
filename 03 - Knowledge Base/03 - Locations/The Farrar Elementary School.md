# Farrar Elementary School
**Location**: 11124 NE 134th Way, Maxwell (Farrar), Iowa
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Abandoned Historic Schoolhouse
**Investigated by**: Ghost Adventures (Travel Channel), My Ghost Story (Biography Channel), Destination Fear, and [[Sam and Colby]].
## History:
- #### Consolidation Era (1921–1922):
	- Built to consolidate several rural one-room country schoolhouses into a singular, modern, three-story brick educational facility for the farming community of Farrar. 
- #### The Dedication:
	- Officially opened on May 1, 1923, featuring state-of-the-art classrooms, a gymnasium, a theater stage, and a boiler room system.
- #### The Closure (2001): 
	- Due to a declining rural population and school district restructuring, the school shut its doors permanently after 78 continuous years of operation.
- #### Private Purchase (2006): 
	- Purchased by Jim and Nancy Oliver, who originally intended to use the property as a private residence but pivoted to historical preservation after experiencing extreme paranormal activity.
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Phantom School Bells: 
	- The sound of disembodied recess and class bells echoing through the vacant corridors despite the physical bell system being long disconnected.
- #### Shadow Figures: 
	- Towering, dark human silhouettes standing in doorways and slinking through the third-floor auditorium space.
- #### Poltergeist Apparitions: 
	- Sightings of ghostly children running up and down the stairwells, accompanied by the sounds of giggling and slamming locker doors.
- #### Physical Boundary Violations: 
	- Investigators reporting being pushed on the stairs, grabbed on the arms, or experiencing sudden localized nausea.

	- A residual spirit of a young female student heard crying out or sobbing in the second-floor restrooms.

## Important Events:
- #### 1923 - The Grand Opening: 
	- The building serves as the absolute social and educational epicenter for generations of families in the town of Farrar.
- #### 2006 - The Haunting Discovery:
	- Immediately after purchasing the building, the Olivers were greeted on their very first day by a full-bodied apparition of a small boy standing on the stairs, prompting decades of ghost research.
## Recurring Elements:
- #### The Third Floor Center: 
	- The undisputed epicenter of negative or aggressive activity, where temperatures fluctuate instantly and light anomalies are routinely filmed.
- #### Residual Activity Patterns: 
	- The building behaves like a time capsule, repeating the ambient sounds of a bustling school day (chatter, running feet) at random intervals during nighttime lockdowns.
