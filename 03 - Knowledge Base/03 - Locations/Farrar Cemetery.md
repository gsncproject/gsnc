- #### Pioneer Foundation (1850s): 
	- Established over 150 years ago as a small, rural burying ground to serve the early farming pioneers and settlers of Washington Township in Polk County, Iowa.

	- Positioned immediately adjacent to the historic site where the 1889 Buena Vista Church originally stood, which was later replaced by the Farrar Methodist Church. 
- #### The Present Day: 
	- The cemetery remains an active but highly protected historical landmark. While the nearby village has dwindled, the graveyard stands intact surrounded by open Iowa cornfields.
