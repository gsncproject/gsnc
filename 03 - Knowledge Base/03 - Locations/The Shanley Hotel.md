# The Shanley Hotel
**Location**: 56 Main Street, Napanoch, Ulster County, New York
**Country**: United States
**Type**: Historic Bed & Breakfast / Active Paranormal Hotel
**Investigated by**: Ghost Hunters (Syfy), Ghost Lab (Discovery), Kindred Spirits (Travel Channel), [[Sam and Colby]], and thousands of independent public/private research teams.
## History:
- #### The Initial Stays (1845–1851): 
	- Originally opened by [[Thomas Ritch]] as a highly successful transit hotel catering to travelers using the nearby Delaware and Hudson Canal and regional railroad networks. 
- #### The Great Fire (1895): 
	- Under the ownership of [[Adolph Wagner]], a nearby structural fire completely leaped onto the hotel property, burning the original building entirely to the ground. Wagner rebuilt the current Victorian framework within a year.
- #### The Shanley Era (1906–1937): 
	- Purchased by Irish immigrant [[James Shanley]]. He added a massive rear attachment housing a local barbershop on the floor level and a clandestine second-story bordello/gentlemen's lounge. During Prohibition, it functioned as a prominent local speakeasy utilizing hidden brick crawlspaces.
- #### Abandonment and Revival: 
	- Following decades of rotating owners, the property sat completely abandoned and rotting from 1991 to 2005. It was rescued by [[Salvatore Nicosia]], who restored the building and pivoted entirely to paranormal preservation tourism. 
## Alleged Activity:
- #### Automated Furniture: 
	- Highly documented accounts of vintage rocking chairs forcefully rocking by themselves on empty floorboards.
- #### Phantom Scent Blasts: 
	- Pungent, isolated bursts of fresh floral perfume or the rich aroma of homestyle food cooking late at night when the kitchen is completely locked and vacant.
- #### Disembodied Chimes: 
	- The acoustic sound of vintage clocks striking the hour inside rooms where no physical clock exists.
- #### Physical Alterations: 
	- Investigators step into the old upper bordello area and report instant bouts of lightheadedness, sudden shortness of breath, and an overwhelming pressure on the chest.
